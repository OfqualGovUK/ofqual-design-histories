Ofqual's design histories site is a record of our user research and interaction design decisions behind the creation of Ofqual's digital services.

It's purpose is to capture why design choices were made and which user research insights led to the design of components, patterns and services.


## Why we are doing it

1. Documenting decision-making – providing transparency for stakeholders and to link design choices back to user needs, constraints and best practices
2. Knowledge sharing – to allow other teams across UK government, who may be designing similar services, to see our research and design choices and to act as a reference for new team members
3. Supporting iterative design – to learn from past iterations and avoid repeating mistakes, to encourage design based on evidence and to avoid duplication of effort
